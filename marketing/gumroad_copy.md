Long description
InkBloom MaxPack is a carefully curated toolkit for creators who paint botanical art in Procreate. The pack includes 60 brushes across 11 families (Fine Nib Inkers, Root & Vine, Bloom Washes, Pressed Petals, Stipple & Texture, Growth Splatter, Smudge & Blend, Botanical Erasers, Detail Overlays, Precision Lines, Colored Pencils) plus full-canvas textures and the grain/shape assets used to build the set.

Install instructions (for buyers)
1. Download InkBloom-MaxPack-Full.zip on your iPad or computer.
2. Unzip the package (on iPad use the Files app or unzip on desktop and transfer to iPad).
3. Open Procreate → Brush Library → tap the “+” or menu → Import → select `InkBloom-MaxPack.brushset` to install.
4. To use background textures: open or create a canvas → Actions (wrench icon) → Add → Insert a photo → choose `linen_field_journal.png` or `aged_herbarium_page.png` and send to back.
