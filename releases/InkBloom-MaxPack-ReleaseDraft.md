Release title:
InkBloom MaxPack — 60 Botanical Brushes for Procreate

Release notes (draft):

InkBloom MaxPack is a hand-crafted collection of 60 brushes optimized for botanical illustration in Procreate. The pack includes:

- 11 families of brushes (Fine Nib Inkers, Root & Vine, Bloom Washes, Pressed Petals, Stipple & Texture, Growth Splatter, Smudge & Blend, Botanical Erasers, Detail Overlay Textures, Precision Lines, Colored Pencils)
- All shared grain and shape assets for consistent texture and behavior
- Canvas background textures: Linen Field Journal and Aged Herbarium Page

How to install:
1. Download `InkBloom-MaxPack-Full.zip` and unzip on your computer or iPad.
2. Import `InkBloom-MaxPack.brushset` into Procreate via the brush library > import.
3. Place the canvas textures into your Procreate gallery or import them into the canvas as a background layer.

Notes for buyers:

- Each brush includes a short usage tip in "About This Brush".
